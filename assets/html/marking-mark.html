Marking-Up Mark
Mark was the first, but was it also the worst?
Despite the order of the gospels in the New Testament, Mark is actually the earliest one.
Reading the gospels in the order in which they were written shows a clear progression in how Jesus is portrayed.
Mark~65–70 CE
the earliest narrative gospel
Matthew~80–90 CE
edits and expands Mark
It contains about 80-90% of Mark's material
Luke~80–90 CE
edits and expands Mark (and possibly Matthew)
It contains about 50-60% of Mark's material
John~90–100 CE
mostly independent

Bottom Line
Was Jesus just a man, or was he God? The most human portrayal of Jesus: no divine pedigree, limited power and knowledge, and openly emotional A clear elevation in divine status and overall behavior, but he's still emotional as the end draws near Similar to Matthew's Jesus, but here he's much less emotional in Gethsemane and on the cross This Jesus seems barely human: he has no limitations, and he's continuously stoic and resolute, especially in the face of death
